- Fault level at a sub-board through its feeder, IEC 60909-0 (cmax 1.10); breaking capacity at or above it.
- Earth fault disconnection, IEC 60364-4-41 §411.4.4: Zs x Ia <= 0.95 U0, Ia the top of the breaker's instantaneous band (IEC 60898-1).
- Short circuit along the cable, ABB §2.4: the far-end Ikmin trips the breaker at once; k²S² from Table 1 for comparison with the breaker's let-through I²t.
