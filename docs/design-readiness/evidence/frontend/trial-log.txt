Leave 1 spare outgoing way (20 %).
Loads without a power factor were taken at cos φ 0.9.
Each breaker is rated at least 1.6 × the largest breaker after it, so they discriminate on overload. On short circuit, miniature breakers discriminate only up to the upstream breaker’s instantaneous trip (about 5 × In for curve C); where total selectivity is required, confirm it from the manufacturer’s selectivity tables.
4 circuits have no cable length given; their voltage drop is not checked.
